## Finding: the published LSTM was tuned at 5x but run at 10x

- The published tuning sweep (`lstm_tuning_seeds101-105/`) ran at SHIFT_SCALE 5x: seed 101, setting 1e-2,600,16, matches it exactly at 5 and at no other scale tried (1-4, 6, 8, 10; `shift1x/probe/tune_probe.py`, `t_x*.log`).
- The published tuned runs (`s20_cal{3,10}_s*_lstmtuned`) ran at 10x (exact seed-1 match at 10 only).
- So the setting picked on the tuning seeds was then used at a different scale from the one it was tuned at. In the 1x reruns, tuning and the tuned runs share one scale (1x), and the pick is preregistered (`shift1x/PREREG_lstm_pick.md`).
